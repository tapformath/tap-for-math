I want to make math accessible to every student willing to step outside their comfort zone, starting with a tap class!

FAQ

Do I need to be good at tap dance?

Not at all! You just need to attend a class. Beginner classes count. The goal is to try something new and have fun.

What if there's no tap class near me?

Many community centers and dance studios offer beginner tap. Online tap classes also count.

How long is a math session?

Each session is 30-45 minutes, one-on-one over Zoom. We'll focus on whatever you need help with.

Is this really free?

Yes! 100% free. My only ask is that you genuinely attend a tap class first. It's about building discipline and trying new things.

What grades/levels do you tutor?

I can help with middle school through high school math, including AP courses and standardized test prep.
